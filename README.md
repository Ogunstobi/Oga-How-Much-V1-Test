# Oga, How Much?

A lightweight Nigerian bargaining calculator for turning a seller's asking price into a personal offer, showing potential savings, and celebrating when oga finally gree.

## V1

1. Enter the seller's asking price — **How much you dem call am?**
2. Enter the buyer's willing price — **How much dey your hand?**
3. See the price difference, percentage knocked off, and potential saving.
4. Tell the app whether **Oga Gree** or **Oga No Gree**.
5. If oga agrees, the potential saving becomes an actual saving.

## Scope

This V1 has no database, backend, authentication, external pricing API, market-price lookup, or saved history. Calculations happen locally in the browser.
